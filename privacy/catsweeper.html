Catsweeper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Catsweeper is a game for Android in which you try to finish tasks on your laptop while a cat keeps ruining it. This page explains what information the app uses and why. The short version: your progress stays on your own phone, there are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not collect anything about you.

Who we are

The app is made by devbydima. You can reach us at devbydima@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (Cat Money, bought items) and your settings (sound and vibration) are stored on your phone. We never receive them. If you use Android's backup feature, your phone may include this data in your Google backup; that backup belongs to your Google account and is handled by Google, not by us. If you uninstall the app or reset your progress, the data is removed from your phone.

What we do not do

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads and no ad networks in the app.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

The fake apps in the game

The browser, mail and other programs you see on the laptop screen are part of the game. They are not real: they do not connect to real websites or accounts, and anything you type there stays in the game and is not sent anywhere.

Sound and vibration

The app plays sounds and can vibrate your phone as part of the game. It does not use your microphone. You can switch both off in the settings.

Age

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, no special data is collected from children either. If you have a question about this, contact us and we will help.

How long information is kept

We keep nothing: all game data is on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about privacy, write to us at the address above.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, for example the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

US state privacy laws

We do not sell personal information and we do not collect personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
