What Changed? — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

What Changed? is a memory game for Android in which you look at a picture and spot what changed. This page explains what information the app uses and why. The short version: the game keeps your progress on your own phone, we have no accounts and no analytics, and the only company that collects information through the app is Google, to show ads.

Who we are

The app is made by devbydima. You can reach us at devbydima@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (stars, scores, stickers, hints, streaks, settings, the language you picked) is stored on your phone. We never receive it. If you use Android’s backup feature, your phone may include this data in your Google backup; that backup belongs to your Google account and is handled by Google, not by us. If you uninstall the app or choose Reset progress in the settings, it is removed from your phone.

What we do not do

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking of our own, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • We do not sell your information.

Sharing

The share buttons open your phone’s share menu. Sharing only happens when you tap a button and pick an app yourself. A picture you share is kept briefly in the app’s temporary cache on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: videos you choose to watch in return for a reward (such as a hint or an extra try), and now and then a full-screen ad between levels. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this information. How Google handles it is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and in Google’s Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google’s consent form. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Legal basis and Google’s role (EEA, UK)

We do not process personal information ourselves. For the advertising information described above, Google acts as an independent controller, and the legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give or refuse in Google’s consent form. You can withdraw it at any time under Settings → Privacy options. Google may process this information in countries outside the EEA and the UK, under the safeguards described in Google’s own policies.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone at the time you chose. It is created by the app itself on your phone: nothing is sent from a server, and no information leaves your device for this. You can turn it off in the settings or in your phone’s notification settings at any time.

Age

What Changed? is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will help.

How long information is kept

We keep nothing: all game data is on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app. Information that Google collects for ads is kept by Google under its own policy.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information that Google holds, you can use the tools at myaccount.google.com. If you have a question about privacy, write to us at the address above.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to withdraw your consent (see above) and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, for example the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

US state privacy laws

We do not sell personal information and we do not collect personal information ourselves. Advertising information processed by Google through the app may count as “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under some US state laws. You can opt out of personalised ads in the app under Settings → Privacy options (where shown) or in your phone’s ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app’s update notes.
